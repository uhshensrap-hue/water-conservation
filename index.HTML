Water is precious, save it for our future generation.

Why is water Important?

Water is essential for all living things. We need water for drinking, cooking, farming and daily activities.

Ways to save water

1.Turn off taps

Turn off taps when not in use. Many people waste water by keeping taps open. We should close taps properly after using water.

2.Fix Leaking Taps

Repair Leaking taps to prevent water waste. Leaking taps waste a lot of water everyday. We should repair damaged taps as soon as possible.

4.Rainwater Harvesting

Collect rainwater and use it wisely.This is a good way to save water. We should collect rainwater in tanks and use it for other activities

4.Reuse water

We should reuse water to save water. We can use leftover water for plants and cleaning. Reusing water help to reduce water wastage
